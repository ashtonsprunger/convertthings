Understanding Time Conversions

The standard SI base unit of time is the second (s), calibrated against cesium-133 hyperfine resonance.

Supported Time Units (12 Units)

  • Second (s) — Primary SI / Base Reference Unit
  • Millisecond (ms) — 1 ms = 0.001 s
  • Microsecond (µs) (Microseconds) — 1 µs = 0.000001 s
  • Nanosecond (ns) (Nanoseconds) — 1 ns = 0 s
  • Minute (min) — 1 min = 60 s
  • Hour (h) — 1 h = 3600 s
  • Day (d) — 1 d = 86400 s
  • Week (wk) — 1 wk = 604800 s
  • Month (Average) (mo) — 1 mo = 2629800 s
  • Year (365.25 days) (yr) — 1 yr = 31557600 s
  • Decade (decade) (Decades (10 years)) — 1 decade = 315576000 s
  • Century (century) (Centuries (100 years)) — 1 century = 3155760000 s

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions and verified calculations for time units.

How many hours are in a year?

In a standard 365-day year, there are 8,760 hours. In a 366-day leap year, there are 8,784 hours.

How many seconds are in a day?

There are exactly 86,400 seconds in a 24-hour day (24 × 60 × 60).
