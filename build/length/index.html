Understanding Length & Distance Conversions

The standard SI base unit of length is the meter (m), defined as the distance traveled by light in a vacuum in 1/299,792,458 of a second.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions and verified calculations for length & distance units.

How many miles are in a kilometer?

There are approximately 0.621371 miles in 1 kilometer. To convert kilometers to miles quickly in your head, multiply by 6 and divide by 10 (or multiply by 0.6).

How do I convert inches to centimeters?

Multiply the length in inches by 2.54. For example, 10 inches is equal to 25.4 centimeters.

What is the difference between statute miles and nautical miles?

A statute mile (land mile) is 5,280 feet or 1,609.344 meters. A nautical mile is based on one minute of latitude on Earth and is standardized as 1,852 meters (approx. 6,076.12 feet).
