Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions and verified calculations for fuel economy units.

How do I convert MPG to L/100km?

Divide 235.215 by the US MPG number. For example, 30 MPG equals approx 7.84 L/100km.

Why is UK MPG different from US MPG?

An Imperial (UK) gallon is 4.546 liters, while a US gallon is 3.785 liters. Therefore, a vehicle achieves ~20% higher MPG in the UK for the exact same efficiency.
