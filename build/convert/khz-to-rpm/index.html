Unit Definitions & Metrology Context

The standard SI unit of frequency is the hertz (Hz), defined as one complete cycle or oscillation per second (1 s⁻¹).

About Kilohertz (kHz): Kilohertz (kHz) is a unit in the Frequency measurement category. It is widely used in audio & am radio (1,000 hz). Relative to the frequency base unit, 1 kHz equals 1000 Hertz (Hz).

About Revolutions per Minute (RPM): Revolutions per Minute (RPM) is a unit in the Frequency measurement category. It is widely used in motor & engine rotation. Relative to the frequency base unit, 1 RPM equals 0.016667 Hertz (Hz).

Frequently Asked Questions

Verified answers and formulas for converting kilohertz to revolutions per minute.

How many revolutions per minute are in 1 kilohertz?

There are 60000 revolutions per minute (RPM) in 1 kilohertz (kHz). Conversely, 1 revolutions per minute equals 0.000017 kilohertz.

How do you convert kilohertz to revolutions per minute?

To convert kilohertz (kHz) to revolutions per minute (RPM), use the formula: RPM = kHz × 60000. Multiply the kilohertz value by 60000. For example, 10 kHz equals 600000 RPM.

How do I convert back from revolutions per minute to kilohertz?

To convert in the reverse direction from revolutions per minute (RPM) to kilohertz (kHz), use the inverse formula: kHz = RPM / 60000. Divide the revolutions per minute value by 60000 (1 RPM = 0.000017 kHz).

What is the difference between kilohertz (kHz) and revolutions per minute (RPM)?

Both kilohertz and revolutions per minute measure frequency. Kilohertz (kHz) is a unit in the Frequency measurement category. It is widely used in audio & am radio (1,000 hz). Relative to the frequency base unit, 1 kHz equals 1000 Hertz (Hz). Revolutions per Minute (RPM) is a unit in the Frequency measurement category. It is widely used in motor & engine rotation. Relative to the frequency base unit, 1 RPM equals 0.016667 Hertz (Hz).

How do you convert RPM to Hertz (Hz)?

To convert RPM (revolutions per minute) to Hertz (cycles per second), divide by 60. For example, a car engine idling at 600 RPM is spinning at 10 Hz (600 ÷ 60 = 10 Hz).
