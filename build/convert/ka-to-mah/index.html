Unit Definitions & Metrology Context

The standard SI base unit of electric current is the ampere (A), defined by taking the fixed numerical value of the elementary charge e to be 1.602176634 × 10⁻¹⁹ coulombs per second.

About Kiloamperes (kA): Kiloampere (kA) is a unit in the Electric Current measurement category. It is widely used in power grids & lightning discharges. Relative to the electric current base unit, 1 kA equals 1000 Amperes (A).

About Milliampere-hours (mAh): Milliampere-hour (mAh) is a unit in the Electric Current measurement category. It is widely used in phone, laptop & power bank battery capacity. Relative to the electric current base unit, 1 mAh equals 0.001 Amperes (A).

Frequently Asked Questions

Verified answers and formulas for converting kiloamperes to milliampere-hours.

How many milliampere-hours are in 1 kiloampere?

There are 1000000 milliampere-hours (mAh) in 1 kiloampere (kA). Conversely, 1 milliampere-hour equals 0.000001 kiloamperes.

How do you convert kiloamperes to milliampere-hours?

To convert kiloamperes (kA) to milliampere-hours (mAh), use the formula: mAh = kA × 1000000. Multiply the kiloampere value by 1000000. For example, 10 kA equals 10000000 mAh.

How do I convert back from milliampere-hours to kiloamperes?

To convert in the reverse direction from milliampere-hours (mAh) to kiloamperes (kA), use the inverse formula: kA = mAh / 1000000. Divide the milliampere-hour value by 1000000 (1 mAh = 0.000001 kA).

What is the difference between kiloampere (kA) and milliampere-hour (mAh)?

Both kiloampere and milliampere-hour measure electric current. Kiloampere (kA) is a unit in the Electric Current measurement category. It is widely used in power grids & lightning discharges. Relative to the electric current base unit, 1 kA equals 1000 Amperes (A). Milliampere-hour (mAh) is a unit in the Electric Current measurement category. It is widely used in phone, laptop & power bank battery capacity. Relative to the electric current base unit, 1 mAh equals 0.001 Amperes (A).

How do you convert mAh (milliampere-hours) to Ah (ampere-hours)?

To convert mAh to Ah, divide the mAh rating by 1,000. For example, a 5,000 mAh smartphone battery or power bank has a capacity of 5 Ah (5,000 ÷ 1,000 = 5 Ah).
