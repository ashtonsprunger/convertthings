Unit Definitions & Metrology Context

The SI unit of volume is the cubic meter (m³). The liter (L) is equal to 1 cubic decimeter (0.001 m³).

About Cubic Centimeters (cm³): Cubic Centimeter (cm³) is a unit in the Volume & Capacity measurement category. It is widely used in 1 ml / 1 cc (engine displacement). Relative to the volume & capacity base unit, 1 cm³ equals 0.001 Liters (L).

About Cubic Feet (ft³): Cubic Foot (ft³) is a unit in the Volume & Capacity measurement category. It is widely used in refrigerators & storage. Relative to the volume & capacity base unit, 1 ft³ equals 28.316847 Liters (L).
