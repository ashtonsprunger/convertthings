Unit Definitions & Metrology Context

The SI unit of illuminance is the lux (lx), which equals one lumen per square meter (1 lx = 1 lm/m²). It quantifies the intensity of light falling on a given surface.

About Phots (ph): Phot (ph) is a unit in the Illuminance measurement category. It is widely used in cgs optical standard (10,000 lux / 1 lm/cm²). Relative to the illuminance base unit, 1 ph equals 10000 Lux (lx).

About Kilolux (klx): Kilolux (klx) is a unit in the Illuminance measurement category. It is widely used in direct sunlight & greenhouse horticulture. Relative to the illuminance base unit, 1 klx equals 1000 Lux (lx).

Frequently Asked Questions

Verified answers and formulas for converting phots to kilolux.

How many kilolux are in 1 phot?

There are 10 kilolux (klx) in 1 phot (ph). Conversely, 1 kilolux equals 0.1 phots.

How do you convert phots to kilolux?

To convert phots (ph) to kilolux (klx), use the formula: klx = ph × 10. Multiply the phot value by 10. For example, 10 ph equals 100 klx.

How do I convert back from kilolux to phots?

To convert in the reverse direction from kilolux (klx) to phots (ph), use the inverse formula: ph = klx / 10. Divide the kilolux value by 10 (1 klx = 0.1 ph).

What is the difference between phot (ph) and kilolux (klx)?

Both phot and kilolux measure illuminance. Phot (ph) is a unit in the Illuminance measurement category. It is widely used in cgs optical standard (10,000 lux / 1 lm/cm²). Relative to the illuminance base unit, 1 ph equals 10000 Lux (lx). Kilolux (klx) is a unit in the Illuminance measurement category. It is widely used in direct sunlight & greenhouse horticulture. Relative to the illuminance base unit, 1 klx equals 1000 Lux (lx).

How do you convert foot-candles (fc) to lux (lx)?

Multiply the foot-candle value by 10.7639. For example, standard office lighting of 50 fc equals approximately 538.2 lux (50 × 10.7639 ≈ 538 lx).
