Unit Definitions & Metrology Context

The standard SI unit of torque is the newton-meter (N·m), defined as the moment resulting from a one-newton force applied perpendicularly to a one-meter moment arm.

About Kilogram-force Meters (kgf·m): Kilogram-force Meter (kgf·m) is a unit in the Torque measurement category. It is widely used in european & japanese legacy specs (9.807 n·m). Relative to the torque base unit, 1 kgf·m equals 9.80665 Newton-meters (N·m).

About Ounce-force Inches (oz-in): Ounce-force Inch (oz-in) is a unit in the Torque measurement category. It is widely used in stepper motors, rc servos & robotics. Relative to the torque base unit, 1 oz-in equals 0.007062 Newton-meters (N·m).
