How many arcseconds are in 1 arcminute?

How do you convert arcminutes to arcseconds?

To convert arcminutes (arcmin) to arcseconds (arcsec), use the formula: arcsec = arcmin × 60. Multiply the arcminute value by 60. For example, 10 arcmin equals 600 arcsec.

How do I convert back from arcseconds to arcminutes?

To convert in the reverse direction from arcseconds (arcsec) to arcminutes (arcmin), use the inverse formula: arcmin = arcsec / 60. Divide the arcsecond value by 60 (1 arcsec = 0.016667 arcmin).
