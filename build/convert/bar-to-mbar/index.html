Unit Definitions & Metrology Context

The SI unit of pressure is the pascal (Pa). Standard sea-level atmosphere is defined as 101,325 Pa (1.01325 bar or ~14.696 psi).

About Bars (bar): Bar (bar) is a unit in the Pressure measurement category. It is a standard everyday unit of pressure measurement. Relative to the pressure base unit, 1 bar equals 100000 Pascals (Pa).

About Millibars (mbar): Millibar (mbar) is a unit in the Pressure measurement category. It is widely used in millibars (barometric pressure). Relative to the pressure base unit, 1 mbar equals 100 Pascals (Pa).

How many millibars are in 1 bar?

There are 1000 millibars (mbar) in 1 bar (bar). Conversely, 1 millibar equals 0.001 bars.

How do you convert bars to millibars?

To convert bars (bar) to millibars (mbar), use the formula: mbar = bar × 1000. Multiply the bar value by 1000. For example, 10 bar equals 10000 mbar.

How do I convert back from millibars to bars?

To convert in the reverse direction from millibars (mbar) to bars (bar), use the inverse formula: bar = mbar / 1000. Divide the millibar value by 1000 (1 mbar = 0.001 bar).
