Unit Definitions & Metrology Context

A circle contains 360 degrees, 2π radians (~6.28318 rad), or 400 gradians.

About Gradians (grad): Gradian (grad) is a unit in the Angle measurement category. It is widely used in gradians (100 per right angle). Relative to the angle base unit, 1 grad equals 0.9 Degrees (°).
