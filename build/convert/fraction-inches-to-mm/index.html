How to Convert Inches to Millimeters (in to mm)

1 Inch (in) is equal to 20.6375 Millimeters (mm). To convert fractional inches to millimeters, divide the fraction numerator by denominator to get decimal inches, then multiply by 25.4 mm. For example: 13 ÷ 16 = 0.8125; 0.8125 × 25.4 = 20.6375 mm.
