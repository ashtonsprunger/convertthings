How to Convert US Cups to US Tablespoons (cup to tbsp)

1 US Cup (cup) is equal to 5 tbsp + 1 tsp US Tablespoons (tbsp). To convert cup fractions to measuring spoons, multiply cups by 16 for tablespoons, or by 48 for teaspoons. For example: 1/3 cup × 16 = 5.33 tbsp (5 tbsp + 1 tsp).
