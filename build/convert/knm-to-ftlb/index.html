Unit Definitions & Metrology Context

The standard SI unit of torque is the newton-meter (N·m), defined as the moment resulting from a one-newton force applied perpendicularly to a one-meter moment arm.

About Kilonewton-meters (kN·m): Kilonewton-meter (kN·m) is a unit in the Torque measurement category. It is widely used in heavy industrial, wind turbines & structural. Relative to the torque base unit, 1 kN·m equals 1000 Newton-meters (N·m).

About Pound-feet (lb-ft): Pound-foot (lb-ft) is a unit in the Torque measurement category. It is widely used in us automotive lug nuts & engine specs. Relative to the torque base unit, 1 lb-ft equals 1.355818 Newton-meters (N·m).
