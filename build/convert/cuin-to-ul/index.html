Unit Definitions & Metrology Context

The SI unit of volume is the cubic meter (m³). The liter (L) is equal to 1 cubic decimeter (0.001 m³).

About Cubic Inches (in³): Cubic Inch (in³) is a unit in the Volume & Capacity measurement category. It is widely used in engine displacement. Relative to the volume & capacity base unit, 1 in³ equals 0.016387 Liters (L).

About Microliters (µL): Microliter (µL) is a unit in the Volume & Capacity measurement category. It is widely used in laboratory pipetting & biology (1 mm³). Relative to the volume & capacity base unit, 1 µL equals 0.000001 Liters (L).
