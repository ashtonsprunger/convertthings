How many years are in 1 decade?

There are 10 years (yr) in 1 decade (decade). Conversely, 1 year (365.25 days) equals 0.1 decades.

How do you convert decades to years?

To convert decades (decade) to years (yr), use the formula: yr = decade × 10. Multiply the decade value by 10. For example, 10 decade equals 100 yr.

How do I convert back from years to decades?

To convert in the reverse direction from years (yr) to decades (decade), use the inverse formula: decade = yr / 10. Divide the year (365.25 days) value by 10 (1 yr = 0.1 decade).
