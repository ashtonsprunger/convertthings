Unit Definitions & Metrology Context

The SI unit of pressure is the pascal (Pa). Standard sea-level atmosphere is defined as 101,325 Pa (1.01325 bar or ~14.696 psi).

About Torr (Torr): Torr / mmHg (Torr) is a unit in the Pressure measurement category. It is widely used in torr / mmhg (vacuum & blood pressure). Relative to the pressure base unit, 1 Torr equals 133.322368 Pascals (Pa).

About Kilopounds per Square Inch (ksi): Kilopound per Square Inch (ksi) is a unit in the Pressure measurement category. It is widely used in 1,000 psi (steel tensile strength). Relative to the pressure base unit, 1 ksi equals 6894757.293168 Pascals (Pa).
