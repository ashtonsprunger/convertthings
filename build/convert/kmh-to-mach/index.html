Unit Definitions & Metrology Context

The SI base unit of speed is the meter per second (m/s). 1 knot equals 1 nautical mile per hour (1.852 km/h).

About Kilometers per Hour (km/h): Kilometer per Hour (km/h) is a unit in the Speed measurement category. It is a standard everyday unit of speed measurement. Relative to the speed base unit, 1 km/h equals 0.277778 Meters per Second (m/s).

About Mach (Ma): Mach (Standard Sea Level) (Ma) is a unit in the Speed measurement category. It is widely used in speed of sound (sea level). Relative to the speed base unit, 1 Ma equals 340.29 Meters per Second (m/s).
