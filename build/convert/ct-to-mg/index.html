Unit Definitions & Metrology Context

The standard SI base unit of mass is the kilogram (kg), defined in terms of the Planck constant (h = 6.62607015 × 10⁻³⁴ J⋅s).

About Carats (ct): Carat (ct) is a unit in the Weight & Mass measurement category. It is widely used in diamonds & gemstones. Relative to the weight & mass base unit, 1 ct equals 0.0002 Kilograms (kg).

About Milligrams (mg): Milligram (mg) is a unit in the Weight & Mass measurement category. It is a standard everyday unit of weight & mass measurement. Relative to the weight & mass base unit, 1 mg equals 0.000001 Kilograms (kg).

How many milligrams are in 1 carat?

How do you convert carats to milligrams?

To convert carats (ct) to milligrams (mg), use the formula: mg = ct × 200. Multiply the carat value by 200. For example, 10 ct equals 2000 mg.

How do I convert back from milligrams to carats?

To convert in the reverse direction from milligrams (mg) to carats (ct), use the inverse formula: ct = mg / 200. Divide the milligram value by 200 (1 mg = 0.005 ct).
