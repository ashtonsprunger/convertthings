Unit Definitions & Metrology Context

The standard SI unit of frequency is the hertz (Hz), defined as one complete cycle or oscillation per second (1 s⁻¹).

About Radians per Second (rad/s): Radians per Second (rad/s) is a unit in the Frequency measurement category. It is widely used in angular velocity (ω = 2πf). Relative to the frequency base unit, 1 rad/s equals 0.159155 Hertz (Hz).

About Revolutions per Minute (RPM): Revolutions per Minute (RPM) is a unit in the Frequency measurement category. It is widely used in motor & engine rotation. Relative to the frequency base unit, 1 RPM equals 0.016667 Hertz (Hz).
