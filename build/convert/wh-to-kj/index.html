Unit Definitions & Metrology Context

The SI unit of energy is the joule (J). 1 food calorie (kcal) equals 1,000 small gram-calories, or exactly 4,184 joules.

About Watt-hours (Wh): Watt-hour (Wh) is a unit in the Energy measurement category. It is a standard everyday unit of energy measurement. Relative to the energy base unit, 1 Wh equals 3600 Joules (J).

About Kilojoules (kJ): Kilojoule (kJ) is a unit in the Energy measurement category. It is widely used in kilojoules. Relative to the energy base unit, 1 kJ equals 1000 Joules (J).

Frequently Asked Questions

Verified answers and formulas for converting watt-hours to kilojoules.

How many kilojoules are in 1 watt-hour?

There are 3.6 kilojoules (kJ) in 1 watt-hour (Wh). Conversely, 1 kilojoule equals 0.277778 watt-hours.

How do you convert watt-hours to kilojoules?

To convert watt-hours (Wh) to kilojoules (kJ), use the formula: kJ = Wh × 3.6. Multiply the watt-hour value by 3.6. For example, 10 Wh equals 36 kJ.

How do I convert back from kilojoules to watt-hours?

To convert in the reverse direction from kilojoules (kJ) to watt-hours (Wh), use the inverse formula: Wh = kJ × 0.2777777778. Multiply the kilojoule value by 0.2777777778 (1 kJ = 0.277778 Wh).

What is the difference between watt-hour (Wh) and kilojoule (kJ)?

Both watt-hour and kilojoule measure energy. Watt-hour (Wh) is a unit in the Energy measurement category. It is a standard everyday unit of energy measurement. Relative to the energy base unit, 1 Wh equals 3600 Joules (J). Kilojoule (kJ) is a unit in the Energy measurement category. It is widely used in kilojoules. Relative to the energy base unit, 1 kJ equals 1000 Joules (J).

What is the difference between a calorie and a kilocalorie (kcal)?

A small calorie (cal) heats 1 gram of water by 1°C. A dietary calorie (food calorie / Calorie / kcal) equals 1,000 small calories or 4.184 kJ.
