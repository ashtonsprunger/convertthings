How many fahrenheit are in 1 celsius?

There are 33.8 fahrenheit (°F) in 1 celsius (°C). Conversely, 1 fahrenheit equals -17.222222 celsius.

How do you convert celsius to fahrenheit?

To convert celsius (°C) to fahrenheit (°F), use the formula: °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32. Multiply the Celsius temperature by 1.8 (9/5) and add 32. For example, 10 °C equals 50 °F.

How do I convert back from fahrenheit to celsius?

To convert in the reverse direction from fahrenheit (°F) to celsius (°C), use the inverse formula: °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9. Subtract 32 from the Fahrenheit temperature and multiply by 5/9 (1 °F = -17.222222 °C).

What is the formula to convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?
