How many milliamperes are in 1 abampere (biot)?

There are 10000 milliamperes (mA) in 1 abampere (biot) (abA). Conversely, 1 milliampere equals 0.0001 abamperes.

How do you convert abamperes to milliamperes?

To convert abamperes (abA) to milliamperes (mA), use the formula: mA = abA × 10000. Multiply the abampere (biot) value by 10000. For example, 10 abA equals 100000 mA.

How do I convert back from milliamperes to abamperes?

To convert in the reverse direction from milliamperes (mA) to abamperes (abA), use the inverse formula: abA = mA / 10000. Divide the milliampere value by 10000 (1 mA = 0.0001 abA).

What is the difference between abampere (biot) (abA) and milliampere (mA)?

Both abampere (biot) and milliampere measure electric current. Abampere (Biot) (abA) is a unit in the Electric Current measurement category. It is widely used in 10 amperes (electromagnetic cgs unit). Relative to the electric current base unit, 1 abA equals 10 Amperes (A). How do you convert mAh (milliampere-hours) to Ah (ampere-hours)?
