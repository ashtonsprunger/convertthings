Unit Definitions & Metrology Context

The SI unit of force is the newton (N), defined as the force required to accelerate a mass of one kilogram at one meter per second squared (1 N = 1 kg·m/s²).

About Pounds-force (lbf): Pound-force (lbf) is a unit in the Force measurement category. It is widely used in us engineering standard (1 lb × g). Relative to the force base unit, 1 lbf equals 4.448222 Newtons (N).

About Ounces-force (ozf): Ounce-force (ozf) is a unit in the Force measurement category. It is widely used in precision small mechanisms & springs. Relative to the force base unit, 1 ozf equals 0.278014 Newtons (N).
