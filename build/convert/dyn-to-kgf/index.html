Unit Definitions & Metrology Context

The SI unit of force is the newton (N), defined as the force required to accelerate a mass of one kilogram at one meter per second squared (1 N = 1 kg·m/s²).

About Dynes (dyn): Dyne (dyn) is a unit in the Force measurement category. It is widely used in cgs physics standard (1 g⋅cm/s²). Relative to the force base unit, 1 dyn equals 0.00001 Newtons (N).

About Kilograms-force (kgf): Kilogram-force (kgf) is a unit in the Force measurement category. It is widely used in metric technical unit (kilopond). Relative to the force base unit, 1 kgf equals 9.80665 Newtons (N).

How many kilograms-force are in 1 dyne?

How do you convert dynes to kilograms-force?

To convert dynes (dyn) to kilograms-force (kgf), use the formula: kgf = dyn / 980665. Divide the dyne value by 980665. For example, 10 dyn equals 0 kgf.

How do I convert back from kilograms-force to dynes?

To convert in the reverse direction from kilograms-force (kgf) to dynes (dyn), use the inverse formula: dyn = kgf × 980665. Multiply the kilogram-force value by 980665 (1 kgf = 980665 dyn).

What is the difference between dyne (dyn) and kilogram-force (kgf)?
