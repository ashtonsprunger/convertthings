Unit Definitions & Metrology Context

The standard SI base unit of length is the meter (m), defined as the distance traveled by light in a vacuum in 1/299,792,458 of a second.

About Micrometers (µm): Micrometer (µm) is a unit in the Length & Distance measurement category. It is widely used in microns (cells, fibers). Relative to the length & distance base unit, 1 µm equals 0.000001 Meters (m).

About Mils (mil): Mil (Thou) (mil) is a unit in the Length & Distance measurement category. It is widely used in 0.001 in (pcb traces, sheet metal, paint). Relative to the length & distance base unit, 1 mil equals 0.000025 Meters (m).
