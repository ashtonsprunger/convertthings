Unit Definitions & Metrology Context

In standard digital design and CSS specifications (W3C CSS Values Module), the reference pixel (px) is calibrated to 1/96th of an inch (96 DPI baseline).

About Points (pt): Point (pt) is a unit in the Typography measurement category. It is widely used in 1/72 inch (print, typography & ios font size). Relative to the typography base unit, 1 pt equals 1.333333 Pixels (px).

About Picas (pc): Pica (pc) is a unit in the Typography measurement category. It is widely used in 12 points / 16 px (book & editorial typesetting). Relative to the typography base unit, 1 pc equals 16 Pixels (px).

How many picas are in 1 point?

There are 0.083333 picas (pc) in 1 point (pt). Conversely, 1 pica equals 12 points.

How do you convert points to picas?

To convert points (pt) to picas (pc), use the formula: pc = pt / 12. Divide the point value by 12. For example, 10 pt equals 0.8333 pc.

How do I convert back from picas to points?

To convert in the reverse direction from picas (pc) to points (pt), use the inverse formula: pt = pc × 12. Multiply the pica value by 12 (1 pc = 12 pt).

How do you convert pixels (px) to rem?
