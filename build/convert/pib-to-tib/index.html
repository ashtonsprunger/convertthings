Unit Definitions & Metrology Context

1 Byte equals 8 bits. Decimal storage standards use powers of 1000 (1 GB = 1000 MB), while binary memory uses powers of 1024 (1 GiB = 1024 MiB).

How many tebibytes are in 1 pebibyte (binary)?

There are 1024 tebibytes (TiB) in 1 pebibyte (binary) (PiB). Conversely, 1 tebibyte (binary) equals 0.000977 pebibytes.

How do you convert pebibytes to tebibytes?

To convert pebibytes (PiB) to tebibytes (TiB), use the formula: TiB = PiB × 1024. Multiply the pebibyte (binary) value by 1024. For example, 10 PiB equals 10240 TiB.

How do I convert back from tebibytes to pebibytes?

To convert in the reverse direction from tebibytes (TiB) to pebibytes (PiB), use the inverse formula: PiB = TiB / 1024. Divide the tebibyte (binary) value by 1024 (1 TiB = 0.000977 PiB).

What is the difference between pebibyte (binary) (PiB) and tebibyte (binary) (TiB)?

What is the difference between GB and GiB?

GB (Gigabyte) uses the decimal system (10⁹ = 1,000,000,000 bytes), commonly used by drive manufacturers. GiB (Gibibyte) uses binary (2³⁰ = 1,073,741,824 bytes), commonly used by operating systems.
