Understanding Speed Conversions

The SI base unit of speed is the meter per second (m/s). 1 knot equals 1 nautical mile per hour (1.852 km/h).

Supported Speed Units (6 Units)

  • Meter per Second (m/s) — Primary SI / Base Reference Unit
  • Kilometer per Hour (km/h) — 1 km/h = 0.277778 m/s
  • Mile per Hour (mph) — 1 mph = 0.44704 m/s
  • Knot (kn) (Nautical & aviation speed) — 1 kn = 0.514444 m/s
  • Foot per Second (ft/s) (Ballistics & projectile motion) — 1 ft/s = 0.3048 m/s
  • Mach (Standard Sea Level) (Ma) (Speed of sound (Sea level)) — 1 Ma = 340.29 m/s

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions and verified calculations for speed units.

How do you convert km/h to mph quickly?

Multiply the km/h speed by 0.6. For example, 100 km/h is approximately 60 to 62 mph.

What is a knot in speed?

A knot is a unit of speed equal to one nautical mile per hour, or exactly 1.852 kilometers per hour (~1.151 mph).
