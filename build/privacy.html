Last updated: October 5, 2026
Summary: ConvertThings does not require registration or collect personal identification information. We respect your privacy and only use cookies and standard local storage for functionality and advertising personalization.

ConvertThings ("we", "us", or "our") operates convertthings.com. This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our service.

For more information on how Google collects and processes data, please visit How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

3. Consent Management (EEA, UK, and Switzerland)

For visitors residing in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, we utilize Google's certified Consent Management Platform (CMP). When you first visit the site, you are presented with explicit options to consent, manage your advertising preferences, or decline non-essential cookies in compliance with GDPR and the ePrivacy Directive.

4. California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents have rights regarding their personal data, including the right to opt-out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal data for cross-context behavioral advertising. You can exercise these preferences via our cookie consent controls or through your browser's Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal.

5. Analytics and Server Logs

Like most standard web servers, our hosting infrastructure may collect non-identifiable technical access logs, including internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, internet service provider (ISP), referring/exit pages, platform type, date/time stamp, and number of clicks to analyze trends and administer the site.
