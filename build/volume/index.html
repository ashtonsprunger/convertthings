Understanding Volume & Capacity Conversions

The SI unit of volume is the cubic meter (m³). The liter (L) is equal to 1 cubic decimeter (0.001 m³).

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions and verified calculations for volume & capacity units.

How many cubic feet are in a cubic yard?

There are exactly 27 cubic feet in 1 cubic yard (3 ft × 3 ft × 3 ft = 27 ft³). To convert cubic feet to cubic yards, divide by 27.

How much volume is in a cubic yard of dirt or mulch?

A cubic yard measures 3 feet wide by 3 feet long by 3 feet deep (27 cubic feet, or approx. 764.55 liters). It is the standard commercial measure used for buying bulk topsoil, gravel, mulch, and ordering ready-mix concrete.

How many cups are in a gallon?

In the US customary system, 1 US gallon contains 16 cups (4 quarts = 8 pints = 16 cups).

What is the difference between a US gallon and an Imperial gallon?

A US liquid gallon is 3.78541 liters (128 US fl oz), while an Imperial (UK) gallon is 4.54609 liters (160 Imperial fl oz). The Imperial gallon is about 20% larger.
