Understanding Pressure Conversions

The SI unit of pressure is the pascal (Pa). Standard sea-level atmosphere is defined as 101,325 Pa (1.01325 bar or ~14.696 psi).

Supported Pressure Units (13 Units)

  • Pascal (Pa) (Base SI unit (N/m²)) — Primary SI / Base Reference Unit
  • Kilopascal (kPa) — 1 kPa = 1000 Pa
  • Megapascal (MPa) (Megapascals (Materials engineering)) — 1 MPa = 1000000 Pa
  • Bar (bar) — 1 bar = 100000 Pa
  • Millibar (mbar) (Millibars (Barometric pressure)) — 1 mbar = 100 Pa
  • Pound per Square Inch (psi) — 1 psi = 6894.757293 Pa
  • Kilopound per Square Inch (ksi) (1,000 psi (Steel tensile strength)) — 1 ksi = 6894757.293168 Pa
  • Standard Atmosphere (atm) — 1 atm = 101325 Pa
  • Torr / mmHg (Torr) (Torr / mmHg (Vacuum & blood pressure)) — 1 Torr = 133.322368 Pa
  • Inch of Mercury (inHg) (Inches of Mercury (Aviation altimeters)) — 1 inHg = 3386.389 Pa
  • Hectopascal (hPa) (Hectopascals (Meteorology)) — 1 hPa = 100 Pa
  • Inch of Water (inH₂O) (HVAC duct & fan static pressure) — 1 inH₂O = 249.08891 Pa
  • Centimeter of Water (cmH₂O) (CPAP & medical respiratory pressure) — 1 cmH₂O = 98.0665 Pa

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions and verified calculations for pressure units.

How many PSI is in 1 bar?

1 bar equals approximately 14.5038 PSI. For car tires, 2.2 bar is roughly 32 PSI.

What is standard atmospheric pressure?

Standard atmospheric pressure at sea level is 1 atm, equal to 101.325 kPa, 1.01325 bar, or 14.696 PSI.
