Understanding Temperature Conversions

The Kelvin (K) is the primary thermodynamic temperature scale, where 0 K represents absolute zero.

Supported Temperature Units (4 Units)

  • Celsius (°C) — Primary SI / Base Reference Unit
  • Fahrenheit (°F) — 1 °F = -17.222222 °C
  • Kelvin (K) — 1 K = -272.15 °C
  • Rankine (°R) (Thermodynamics (Absolute °F)) — 1 °R = -272.594444 °C

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions and verified calculations for temperature units.

What is the formula to convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

The formula is: °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32. For example, 100°C × 1.8 + 32 = 212°F.

What is the formula to convert Fahrenheit to Celsius?

The formula is: °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9. For example, (68°F − 32) × 5/9 = 20°C.

At what temperature are Celsius and Fahrenheit equal?

Celsius and Fahrenheit read the exact same number at -40 degrees (-40°C = -40°F).
